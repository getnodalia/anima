Ánima app icon (anima.svg and every file derived from it in this directory: anima-1024.png,
anima-padded-1024.png, anima-macos-512.png, anima.ico, hicolor/).

The source photograph of Émile Cohl (unidentified photographer, circa 1885) is in the public
domain: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Emile_Cohl_portrait.jpg

The icon made from it (the cut-out, black-and-white portrait on Ánima's violet tile) is licensed

at your option.

Regenerate the derived files with scripts/icons.sh (see README.md).
